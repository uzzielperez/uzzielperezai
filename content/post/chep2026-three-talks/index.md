I presented three talks at [CHEP 2026](https://indico.cern.ch/), the 28th International Conference on Computing in High Energy and Nuclear Physics, hosted by Chulalongkorn University in Bangkok, Thailand (May 25–29, 2026):

1. **When Less is More** — lightweight and distilled graph neural networks for efficient particle reconstruction in LHCb's next-generation PicoCal calorimeter.
2. **Optimizing GNNs for the Wild** — PyTorch-to-ONNX acceleration of GarNet on CPUs and GPUs, targeting the LHCb GPU trigger.
3. **Future-Ready Restoration** — a case study on RAG-enhanced, agentic revival of a Run-2 Λb → Λγ analysis.

Details for each talk are on the [talks page](/event/).
