At the University of Alabama I led weekly recitation and laboratory sessions for honors-track calculus-based Electromagnetism, about 100 undergraduate students per semester (2016–2019 and 2022–2024). I was also selected as primary instructor for *Physics for Physics Teachers*, training future secondary-education instructors, and served as summer lecturer for calculus-based Electromagnetism in 2019.
