Core contributor to **ecal-reco-gnn**, the LHCb calorimeter reconstruction package that hosts **UzzieNet**: an attention-enhanced, node-centric graph neural network extending GarNet. Custom graph construction, message-passing and attention layers, model compression, and ONNX deployment target the PicoCal (Upgrade II) calorimeter and the GPU-based HLT1/Allen trigger.

Presented at [ACAT 2025](/event/acat2025-lightweight-gnns/), [Fast ML 2025](/event/fastml2025-lightweight-gnns/), and [CHEP 2026](/event/chep2026-lightweight-gnns/).
