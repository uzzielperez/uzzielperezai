# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- Frontier AI lab recruiters and research scientists (Anthropic, DeepMind, OpenAI, etc.) screening a research-engineer/scientist candidate in about 90 seconds, usually on a laptop during work hours.
- ERC and other grant-panel reviewers checking that the applicant's research program, publications, talks, and outreach are real and coherent.
- Physics and ML collaborators deciding whether to reach out.

The job to be done: answer "is this person's work real, distinctive, and relevant to me?" fast, with evidence, not adjectives.

## Product Purpose

Personal academic site of Uzziel Perez, postdoctoral researcher in AI for high-energy physics (DS4DS, La Salle Campus Barcelona). It exists to convert a skim into a follow-up: an interview, a grant shortlist, a collaboration email. Success = a visitor can retell three concrete things she built within one scroll.

## Brand Personality

Warm, rigorous, playful-precise. A physicist who builds real systems and communicates science visually. Evidence-first: every claim carries a number, an artifact, or a talk. Never salesy, never template-generic.

## Anti-references

- Untouched academic-theme template pages (lorem ipsum, demo posts, "Your Name" navbars).
- SaaS landing-page clichés: hero metrics with gradient accents, identical card grids.
- Buzzword soup without artifacts ("passionate about AI").

## Design Principles

1. Evidence over adjectives: quantified outcomes (8x faster inference, ~95% smaller models) next to every skill claim.
2. Physics-native visual language: detector and event-display metaphors used where they carry meaning, not decoration.
3. One-scroll credibility: research, skills, publications, talks reachable without hunting.
4. Never fabricate: every fact traces to the CV fact-bank or user-confirmed sources; unverified items are flagged, not invented.
5. Show, don't tell: interactive visualizations (aurora, black hole, skills tracks) demonstrate the craft the text describes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in both light and dark themes. All animation respects prefers-reduced-motion (WebGL and SVG draw-in effects included). Interactive visualizations degrade to readable static content without JavaScript.
